Arresalah Institute

Our logo stands for a learning home where students are guided with sincerity, structure, and love for the Book of Allah.

Mission

Nurturing Hearts Through the Qur'an

ArResalah Institute exists to provide structured, sincere, and disciplined Qur'an memorization rooted in ikhlas, correct tajweed, and sound Islamic manners, raising students who live by the Qur'an before reciting it.

Vision

A Generation Anchored to Guidance

To raise a generation whose hearts are anchored to the Qur'an, whose tongues recite it with excellence, and whose lives reflect its guidance as sources of light, integrity, and leadership in their homes and communities.

Privacy Policy

How We Protect Your Information

Last updated: June 7, 2026. Arresalah Institute for Qur'an Memorization respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through our website and enrollment portal.

Information We Collect

We collect the details you submit during enrollment, including your name, phone number, email address, selected course, billing plan, and payment-related application details.

How We Use It

Your information is used to process applications, contact you about classes, provide student support, confirm payment status, and improve our enrollment experience.

Payments

Payments are completed through Paystack. We do not store your card details on this website. Paystack handles payment information according to its own security and privacy practices.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information when needed to operate our classes, process payments, comply with the law, or protect our services.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative steps to protect submitted information, but no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests to update your information, email support@arresalahinstitute.com or contact us on WhatsApp at +234 808 206 2525.
