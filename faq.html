The Apex House is a Muslim women-centered business. Explore our services and booking details for Muslim women.

Who can book The Apex House?
The Apex House is a Muslim women-centered business. All our services, including speaking engagements, event moderation, hosting, consulting, and media, are exclusively for Muslim women.
What services does The Apex House offer?
The Apex House is a Muslim women-centered business. We offer speaking engagements, event moderation, hosting, consulting, and media services exclusively for Muslim women.
Can I book you for a single event?
Yes. Get in touch with your event date, location and the kind of support you need. We’ll discuss availability and a suitable plan.
How do we get started?
Send us a short note about your event or project. We’ll arrange a conversation, learn what matters to you and outline the next steps. You can also book any of our services directly through the website.
Do you support both in-person and online programs?
Yes. Tell us what format you have in mind. We can talk through your audience, program flow and the kind of hosting or moderation that will work best.
How far in advance should I reach out?
As soon as you have a date or an idea taking shape. Early conversations give us more room to plan together; you’re welcome to ask about shorter timelines too.
